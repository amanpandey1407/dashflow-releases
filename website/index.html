ENGINEERING

Speed without compromise.

Designed to maximize your bandwidth, recover stalled connections, and disappear into your everyday workflow.

SPEED ENGINE

Up to 10× faster. Automatically.

Standard browsers download files through a single connection, artificially throttled by remote hosts. DashFlow splits large downloads into simultaneous byte-range chunks across 16 parallel sockets, maxing out your connection throughput effortlessly.

INTELLIGENCE

Real-Time Socket Rebalancing

When an individual connection stutters or hits high latency, idle sockets dynamically take over unfinished ranges. Zero stalled transfers.

Privacy is built in. Not bolted on.

DashFlow operates on a strict Local-First Architecture.

All file downloads, user credentials, and session tokens remain strictly on your local PC. The browser extension talks exclusively to your local desktop daemon (http://127.0.0.1:9527). DashFlow contains zero adware, zero tracking telemetry without consent, and zero third-party miners.
