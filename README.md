## ⚡ Why DashFlow?

DashFlow replaces clunky, ad-filled download managers with a modern, high-performance desktop application designed for power users and everyday speed.

### 🚀 Key Features

* **Multi-Threaded Turbo Engine**: Splits files into up to 16+ simultaneous connections, dynamically redistributing segments to bypass bandwidth throttles and maximize download speeds.
* **1-Click Browser Integration**: Works seamlessly with Chrome, Edge, Brave, and all Chromium browsers. Click any download link in your browser, and DashFlow instantly catches it.
* **Smart Streaming & Media Grabber**: Built-in media engine powered by yt-dlp. Download streaming video and audio with quality and format selection directly from your favorite websites.
* **Intelligent Queue & Scheduler**: Set concurrency limits (e.g. 3 at a time), set speed caps when you need bandwidth for gaming or meetings, or schedule downloads to run automatically overnight.
* **Auto-Categorization**: Automatically sorts completed files into organized folders: *Programs, Videos, Audio, Documents, and Compressed*.
* **Modern & Clean UI**: Premium dark mode, live speed charts, real-time segment progress bars, and unobtrusive system tray minimization.
* **100% Clean & Private**: No ads, no popups, no tracking telemetry, and no third-party bundled toolbars.

## 💻 Download & Installation

### 1. Windows Desktop App
1. **[Download DashFlow-Setup.exe](https://github.com/amanpandey1407/dashflow-releases/releases/latest/download/DashFlow-Setup.exe)** from the latest release.
2. Run the installer and follow the quick setup wizard.
3. Launch **DashFlow** from your Desktop or Start Menu.

### 2. Browser Extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera)
Capture downloads and streaming media from your browser directly into DashFlow:
1. **[Download DashFlow-Extension.zip](https://github.com/amanpandey1407/dashflow-releases/releases/latest/download/DashFlow-Extension.zip)** and extract it to a permanent folder on your PC.
2. Open your browser's extension manager:
   * **Chrome**: `chrome://extensions`
   * **Edge**: `edge://extensions`
   * **Brave**: `brave://extensions`
   * **Opera / Vivaldi**: `opera://extensions`
3. Toggle **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder.
5. The extension is now paired with your DashFlow desktop app!
